FLOWCALI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

FlowCali is an app that analyzes your calisthenics form from video. This policy explains what information the FlowCali iOS app and flowcali.com collect, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address, used to sign you in and identify your account. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider, Clerk; we never see or store it.
  • Workout videos. Videos you record in the app or choose from your photo library to analyze. The app records video only, with no audio.
  • Analysis results. Body-position measurements computed from your videos, your scores, rep counts, coaching feedback, and a skeleton-overlay version of your video.
  • Subscription status. Whether you have an active FlowCali Pro subscription and related purchase events. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your card or payment details.
  • Newsletter (website only). If you sign up for updates on flowcali.com, your email address is stored by our newsletter provider, Beehiiv.

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, or browsing activity, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

How we use it

  • To analyze your form and show you your results, history, and progress.
  • To keep track of your free analyses and your subscription.
  • To keep the service secure and working, for example by rate-limiting requests.

We don't use your videos to train machine-learning models.

How long we keep it

We keep your videos and results until you delete them. Deleting a session in the app removes its videos. Deleting your account removes your account, all of your videos, and all of your analysis results. Copies in our providers' backups are removed on their normal backup schedule.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything associated with it. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

You can also ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing the address below.

Children

FlowCali is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, videos are stored privately and accessed only through short-lived links, and access to our systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top of this page.
